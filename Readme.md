# TGCNSBDC Client-Facing Tools

This repository contains configuration and supporting skills for TGCNSBDC client-facing tools. The tools use AI to help small business owners and SBDC advisors prepare practical business materials.

## Marketing Plan Pro

Marketing Plan Pro guides users through creating, reviewing, or revising a marketing plan tailored to a business’s goals, customers, budget, time, and capacity.

The planning process can cover:

- Priority customer groups and buyer personas
- Market and competitor research
- Positioning, offers, and marketing channels
- Customer journeys from discovery through retention and referral
- A marketing budget and implementation calendar
- Goals, measurement methods, assumptions, and validation items
- Sample content suited to the business and selected channels

The interview has a maximum of 20 questions across the planning process. It skips information already provided, allows users to answer “unknown” or “skip,” and reserves the final question for: “Is there anything additional we should ask?”

## Supporting skills

- **TGCN SBDC - User Checklist:** A staff reminder to review sources, accuracy, relevance, confidentiality, assumptions, and the final content before sharing.
- **TGCNSBDC Branded Documents:** Instructions for preparing branded documents with the network logo and website footer.
- **AI assistance and advisor review:** Marketing Plan Pro’s supporting configuration calls for an appropriate statement in completed deliverables. Human review must not be claimed unless it has actually occurred.

The checklist is intended for staff review. It is not a certification of accuracy, policy compliance, or approval, and it should remain outside client-facing documents unless specifically requested.

## Deliverables

The default deliverable is an editable Word document (`.docx`). A completed marketing plan should include the narrative plan, editable tables, budget, calendar, measures, sources, assumptions, and sample content.

The plan should distinguish verified information from estimates and assumptions. Unknown information should be marked for confirmation. The tool should not invent research, costs, business facts, or results.
